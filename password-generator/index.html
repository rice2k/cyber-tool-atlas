Security notes

What makes a strong password?

Length matters most. For ordinary account passwords, 16-24 random characters is a strong practical range. Longer is better when the service allows it.

Randomness matters. Names, birthdays, keyboard patterns, quotes, and predictable substitutions are easier to guess than genuinely random strings.

Uniqueness matters. Reusing one strong password across several sites still creates risk if any one of those sites is breached.
